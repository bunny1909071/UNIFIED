Health Loan Fast-Track
Get your health loan ready faster
Most delays come from missing or mismatched papers. Build one complete, checked pack, then hand it to the hospital finance desk or lender.
⚠️
UNIFIED AI is not a lender and cannot approve a loan. We only help you prepare. Never pay anyone an "approval fee" and never share your OTP or PIN.
1 · Check cheaper options first
  • Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: free cover at listed hospitals for eligible families. Ask for the hospital's Ayushman Mitra desk or call 14555.
  • State schemes (for example Aarogyasri in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh) and Chief Minister's / Prime Minister's relief funds.
  • Health insurance: ask the hospital to request cashless pre-authorisation before you borrow.
  • Hospital payment plans: many hospitals allow staged payment.
Eligibility and limits change. Confirm with the hospital desk or official government sources.

Privacy notice

What we store

  • Your username, optional email and a hashed password.
  • The reports and results you create, extracted lab values, and the health profile you choose to enter.

How your documents are used

  • Photos, PDFs and text you submit are sent to AI services (Google Gemini and Groq) to produce your result. Avoid including names or ID numbers where you can.
  • If you use Find Clinics, your location is sent to OpenStreetMap services only to look up nearby places. We do not store it.
  • If you use Health Loan, the document you upload is read by AI to fill the form. The form and checklist stay in your browser and are not stored by us.
  • We do not sell your data.

Your choices

  • Download all your data or delete your account at any time from Account.
  • Shared report links are private, expire after 7 days, and can be revoked.

Important

  • Results are AI-generated, for education only, and are not a diagnosis or treatment advice. In an emergency call 112.
